# Farhad Panahov

I am an economist and quantitative researcher in Toronto. Most of my work has been building the analytical layer underneath Canadian public policy research — growth and competitiveness, the energy transition and climate, autos and trade. Usually that means taking a question nobody has good numbers for and finding a way to measure it.

I currently work with RBC's Thought Leadership team and Climate Action Institute, where I author, co-author and contribute to the flagship reports. I studied economics at UBC and hold a master's from MIT.

### What I'm watching in the Canadian economy

[**Nine to Five**](https://panahovf.github.io/nine-to-five/) — How healthy is Canada's labour market? Updated with every Labour Force Survey.

[**Budget Galaxy**](https://panahovf.github.io/budget-galaxy/) — What does the budget say? Every measure in Canada's federal budgets since 2022.

Both are part of [Monitor](https://panahovf.github.io/monitor/), one page per question, each updated when new numbers come out.

### Research and code

[**Choosing Our Future**](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/education/publication/education-for-climate-action) — With the World Bank. Designed the methodology for measuring green employment in developing labour markets and co-authored the green-skills chapter.

[**Carbon budget consistent net zero scenario**](https://github.com/panahovf/ngfsanalysis) — NGFS scenarios applied to power plant emissions and compared against the remaining carbon budget.

[**Air pollution**](https://github.com/panahovf/airpollution) — Economic benefits of reducing air pollution under Net Zero.
